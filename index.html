About

A bit about me

I'm a mechatronics engineering student at Queen's University, drawn to the point where a line of code turns into a physical motion — a motor spinning up, a wheel correcting course, a battery deciding how much current to send where.

Most of my time goes into three things: leading the electrical architecture of an electric go-kart, deploying machine learning models onto microcontrollers with almost no memory to spare, and building small autonomous robots that have to make decisions in real time.

Outside of engineering, I'm a stats enthusiast and a die-hard Leafs fan (still believing).
